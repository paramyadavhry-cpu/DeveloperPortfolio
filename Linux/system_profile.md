## Binary Number Representation

### Decimal 75 to Binary
- 75 ÷ 2 = 37 remainder 1
- 37 ÷ 2 = 18 remainder 1
- 18 ÷ 2 = 9 remainder 0
- 9 ÷ 2 = 4 remainder 1
- 4 ÷ 2 = 2 remainder 0
- 2 ÷ 2 = 1 remainder 0
- 1 ÷ 2 = 0 remainder 1

Answer (remainders read from bottom to top): **1001011**

### Binary 110101 to Decimal

| Bit | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weight | 32 | 16 | 8 | 4 | 2 | 1 |
| Bit x Weight | 32 | 16 | 0 | 4 | 0 | 1 |

Sum: 32 + 16 + 0 + 4 + 0 + 1 = **53**

### Why Computers Use Binary
Computers use binary because their circuits work reliably with two states, ON and OFF, represented by 1 and 0.
